## Finishing Products — Water-Based Only

| Product | Type | Quantity | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Water-based structural epoxy resin | Adhesive (Part A) | Sufficient for 5 frames + 20% buffer | Confirm product approval with security before order |
| Epoxy hardener | Adhesive (Part B) | Matched to resin quantity at correct ratio | Same product family as resin |
| Water-based primer | Finish | 1 litre minimum | White or grey — confirm colour range |
| Water-based topcoat paint | Finish | 250ml per colour minimum; 3–4 colour options | All products on approved product list |
| Water-based clear lacquer (satin) | Finish | 500ml minimum | Confirm VOC level acceptable for setting |

> **CRITICAL** — All finishing products must be confirmed as water-based before procurement. No solvent-based products are permitted in this setting under any circumstances. Check product labels AND data sheets, not just marketing descriptions.

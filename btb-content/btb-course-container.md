# Build to Bond — OCN Level 1 Award in Practical Manufacturing Skills

## COURSE OVERVIEW

> **Programme:** Build to Bond — OCN Level 1 Award in Practical Manufacturing Skills
> Learners design and build a bamboo balance bike from raw materials in a prison workshop setting, developing practical manufacturing skills, teamwork, and confidence. The programme is delivered over two core weeks (10 days) with an optional third rehabilitation week. All evidence is paper-based — no photography by learners is permitted. The finished bikes are donated to children in the local community.

## Course Structure

| Module | Content | Sessions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Module 0: Getting Started** | Preparation guides for the instructor — programme overview, workshop setup, learner profiles, pre-delivery checklist | GS-1 to GS-4 |
| **Module 1: Week 1 (Days 1–5)** | Induction, bamboo science, mini project, balance bike design and bamboo selection | Day 1 to Day 5 |
| **Module 2: Week 2 (Days 6–10)** | Build week — shaping, glue-up, painting, assembly, employment skills and celebration | Day 6 to Day 10 |
| **Module 3: Week 3 OPTIONAL (Days 11–15)** | Rehabilitation module — personalisation, user guide, handover ceremony, presentation | Day 11 to Day 15 |
| **Module 9: Reference Materials** | Checklists, assessment guide, video list, OCN mapping, contingency bank | REF-A to REF-E |

## Day-by-Day Overview

### Module 1 — Week 1: Foundation

| Day | Title | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Workshop Induction & Programme Introduction | Safety rules, tool handling, team building, programme overview |
| Day 2 | Bamboo as a Material | Bamboo science, properties testing, sustainability |
| Day 3 | Mini Bamboo Project — Design & Start Making | Design brief, first hands-on build, basic tool skills |
| Day 4 | Mini Project — Complete & Evaluate + Balance Bike Introduction | Finish mini project, self-evaluation, introduce the balance bike |
| Day 5 | Design Your Balance Bike + Bamboo Selection | Personal design brief, bamboo selection checklist, cutting to length |

### Module 2 — Week 2: Build

| Day | Title | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 6 | Shaping, Fitting & Dry-Fit Assembly | Rasp and file bamboo to fit lugs, sand surfaces, dry-fit approval gate |
| Day 7 | Glue-Up Day | Structural epoxy application, clamping, cure setup |
| Day 8 | De-Jig, Inspect, Sand & Paint | Remove from jig, quality inspection, surface prep, painting |
| Day 9 | Assembly & Final Safety Checks | Fit wheels, handlebars, seat; safety check protocol |
| Day 10 | Employment Skills & Celebration | CV workshop, skills reflection, celebration event, bike handover |

### Module 3 — Week 3: Rehabilitation (Optional)

| Day | Title | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 11 | Who Is This Bike For? | User empathy, community connection, personalisation planning |
| Day 12 | Making It Special | Decals, finishing touches, quality standards |
| Day 13 | User Guide & Personal Message | Write a user guide and personal message to the recipient |
| Day 14 | Presentation Preparation | Prepare presentation of work for handover ceremony |

## Key Information

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Delivery format | 4 hours per day (typically 9:30–12:30 and 1:30–3:30) |
| Cohort size | 5 learners maximum per cohort |
| Accreditation | OCN Level 1 Award in Practical Manufacturing Skills |
| Assessment | Pass / Fail — continuous assessment via paper evidence portfolio |
| Evidence | Paper-based only — workbooks, observation records, signed checklists, instructor photos |
| Setting | Prison workshop — all materials and tools must pass security clearance |
| End product | Bamboo balance bike donated to a child in the local community |

## REFERENCE DOCUMENT

> **Build to Bond**  |  **REF-B**  |  **Assessment Guide for Instructors**
> This guide explains how to gather, record, and submit evidence for the OCN Level 1 Award in Build to Bond. Read before the programme starts.

## Overview

Assessment for this programme uses three methods:

- **Workbook entries** — learners complete written tasks throughout the 10 days

- **Oral Q&A** — instructor asks questions and records responses in observation notes

- **Instructor observation** — instructor records what they see during practical work and signs off at each stage

There is no end test or examination. The portfolio is the qualification.

## Recording Oral Q&A Evidence

When a learner answers an oral question, record the following in your observation notes:

- Date and session

- The question asked (exactly as asked)

- A summary of the learner's response (not word-for-word unless very brief)

- AC reference (e.g. “AC 5.1”)

- Your assessment: PASS or FURTHER EVIDENCE NEEDED

- Your signature

A response that is partially correct may still pass if it demonstrates understanding of the core concept. Use your professional judgement. If in doubt, ask a follow-up question.

## Recording Observation Evidence

For practical observations, record:

- Date and session

- What you observed the learner doing

- Whether it met the required standard

- AC reference

- Your signature

Observation notes should describe what happened, not just “learner completed task correctly.” Example: *“Learner measured and marked tube ends for all 5 components against their specification sheet. All marks confirmed correct before cutting. Cutting technique was safe and controlled. Cut lengths within 2mm of specification on all 5 tubes.”*

## SEND and Literacy Adaptations — Assessment

- **Learners who cannot write:** all workbook responses can be given verbally and scribed by the instructor. Note: “Verbal response, scribed by instructor.”

- **Learners with limited English:** questions can be reworded or simplified. The standard is whether the learner demonstrates understanding, not whether they use technical vocabulary.

- **Learners who cannot complete all ACs on the day:** outstanding ACs can be addressed in later sessions or at final oral assessment on Day 10.

- **Any additional time or support given:** note it on the evidence, but it does not invalidate the evidence. Support during learning is expected and appropriate.

## What Counts as Passing Evidence

| Evidence Type | Minimum to Pass |
| --- | --- |
| Written workbook entry | Addresses the AC in own words. Does not need to be perfect prose. Must show understanding, not just copy the key term definition. |
| Oral Q&A | Answer demonstrates understanding of the core concept. Learner can explain ‘why’ not just ‘what’. |
| Observation record | Instructor confirms the learner carried out the task safely and correctly on at least one occasion. |
| Signed document (COSHH, checklist) | Learner has read and understood the document. Signature alone is not sufficient — there must be accompanying evidence that the content was understood (workbook entry or oral Q&A). |

## Portfolio Submission

- Collect all evidence: workbook, observation notes, signed checklists, COSHH records.

- Organise by LO/AC.

- Ensure every AC has at least one piece of signed evidence.

- Complete the portfolio cover sheet (available from OCN centre contact).

- Submit to OCN centre contact within 5 working days of Day 10.

- Retain copies of all signed documents for 3 years.

## Contact and Queries

- For assessment queries: contact your OCN centre contact before the programme starts, not during.

- For evidence gaps identified mid-programme: address immediately in the next available session. Do not leave gaps for Day 10.

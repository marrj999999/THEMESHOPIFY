## Cross-cutting defects (the CSS-clash layer)

1. **Specificity war between sheets.** bbc-aaa-2026.css (`.rd-wrap p:not(.rd-eyebrow){font-size:var(--t-body)}`) and bbc-mobile-fixes.css (`h1{…!important}` ≤749px) both override bbc-statement.css band styles. This is why the converge title = 18px and mobile hero = 28px. The pathways block already fights back with scattered `!important` (`.rd-path__who`, `.rd-flow__step`) — symptom, not cure. Longer term: exclude `.bbc-rd` pages from the legacy fix sheets (`:not(.bbc-rd *)` or drop the sheets on 2026 templates).
2. **Blanket `.bbc-rd-impact h2{text-transform:lowercase}`** destroys proper nouns in rendered copy: "stem", "makers" (canon: STEM, Makers). Brief rule is "lowercase headlines WITH proper-noun audit" — a transform cannot audit. Remove it; lowercase the copy at source (settings are all editable), keeping STEM/OCN/HMP/Makers.
3. **Three left axes on one page** — x=64 (bands 0–3, 8), x≈254 (band 4), x≈305 (band 7) — plus centered bands 6/9. Brief: one left axis.
4. **Eyebrow system inconsistent**: centered (bands 2/3/6/7/9) vs left (4/5/8), all uppercase vs brief's lowercase. Pick one grammar (left + lowercase per brief) and apply via one class.
5. **Inline `<style>` in bbc-impact-2026.liquid has grown** (grid fix + logo sizing + quote sizing) and still contains a dead `.rd-arms` rule the PATHWAYS-SPEC said to delete (the markup no longer exists). Gate-1: styles land in the consolidated sheet only.
6. **Stray orphan `}` at bbc-statement.css:593** (brace depth goes negative) — harmless today, booby trap tomorrow.
7. **Browser-pane note:** the Claude browser pane renderer repeatedly froze to a flat forest frame after scrolls on this page (DOM fine, paint stuck). Screenshots in this crit come from Puppeteer. If James's phone shows similar mid-scroll flat-forest frames, suspect the scroll-driven `.rd-reveal` animation load — worth one pass with `animation-timeline` support-gating.

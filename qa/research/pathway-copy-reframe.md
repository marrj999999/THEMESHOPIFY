## The finding that matters most: the impact arm contradicts its own destination page

The schools arm links to `/pages/schools`. That page is **already** the general programme James is
asking for:

> H1: *"try real engineering in your classroom this term — free."*
> H2: *"start free. build up when your students ask for more."*

It carries **five engineering strands** — acoustic/product design (the free bamboo Speaker build,
ages 11–16, KS3–KS4, 2–3 hrs), sustainable product engineering, bicycle technical development
(14–18, KS4/post-16), additive manufacturing & adaptive design (14–19), and electric mobility.
It is curriculum-mapped, teacher-led (*"Teachers run this whether or not they've ever held a saw"*)
and free at the front door.

The impact arm reduces all of that to:

> chip *"in schools · before exclusion"* · name *"make engineers"* ·
> who *"for SEND, PRU and at-risk young people being pushed out of the classroom…"*

So the arm is not merely narrow — it describes a **different, smaller offer** than the page it
links to, and targets alternative provision where the destination targets mainstream KS3–post-16
classes. Fixing this is a consistency repair, not just a rewrite.

## Peer evidence (12 UK orgs verified live; unverifiable ones listed at the end)

1. **No comparable UK charity names a schools programme after a downstream career.** Royal Academy
   of Engineering ships *"This is Engineering: Schools"* — a **field** name, not a job-title promise.
   The Design & Technology Association's named products are all process names (D&T Hub Schools,
   Quality Mark). "make engineers" is an outcome claim and an outlier.
2. **Direct precedent for broadening a narrow name:** Sustrans renamed **"Big Pedal" → "Big Walk
   and Wheel"** *because schools told them* the single-activity name undersold what the programme
   had become. Exactly BBC's situation.
3. **Audience specificity belongs in the one-liner, not the title** — Speakers for Schools
   ("11–19-year-old state-educated students"), Nuffield (widening access) both keep generic titles.
4. **Prisons: the sector convention is `[brand] + [activity/skill descriptor]`** — Clink Kitchens,
   Clink Gardens, Barista Academies, HMP Academies — with the emotional value carried in the
   description, never the title. A bond-led title under-claims the vocational content.
5. **Accreditation is stated separately and precisely, never folded into the name.** Clink states
   "accredited City & Guilds NVQs" apart from the brand. BBC's hedged
   *"OCN London bespoke Level 2 … not Ofqual-regulated"* already matches sector best practice —
   a strength to keep, not simplify away.
6. **Flexibility is proved with concrete site lists, not adjectives.** Every verified prison charity
   demonstrates reach by naming prisons (Clink: Brixton, Cardiff, High Down, Styal…; Bounce Back:
   Isis, Wandsworth, Pentonville; Redemption Roasters: High Down, Wandsworth, Downview). **So the
   flexibility claim should name BBC's four active sites rather than say "flexible".**
7. **Make the family strand one named pillar inside a vocational identity** — Key4Life names three
   pillars (Emotional Resilience / Employability / Ongoing Support), Switchback three stages
   (In Prison / In Training / In Work). That is the template for Build to Bond.

*Not verified: Ministry of Furniture (no such org found), The Bike Project (no named schools
programme), halow project (category mismatch — learning disability, not custody), Blue Sky (404),
Onward Lives (403), Timpson academy detail, Greenpower schools page (404), XLP (403).*

## A · Schools arm — general

| Field | Now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| chip | `in schools · before exclusion` | `in schools · ages 11–19` |
| name | `make engineers` | **`real engineering`** |
| who | SEND/PRU/at-risk, before exclusion | `for secondary schools, sixth forms and colleges — five programmes, from a free classroom build to a rideable frame. curriculum-mapped, and built to be teacher-led.` |
| flow | taster → short course → OCN L1 → hand-off | `start free :: the speaker build, 2–3 hours` · `choose a strand :: product, bicycle, additive or e-mobility` · `OCN accredited :: recognised skills, real qualifications` · `teachers run it :: film, session plan and curriculum map included` |
| CTA | `bring it to your school →` | unchanged |

**Why `real engineering`:** it is a *field* descriptor, not an outcome claim (peer point 1), it
mirrors the destination page's own H1 (*"try real engineering in your classroom"*), and it covers
all five strands where "make engineers" covered one implied career. Two words keeps the display
h3 on one line, matching the existing lowercase naming style — and the chip directly above it
already supplies the setting and age range, so the name does not need to repeat "school".

Considered and rejected: `engineering in the classroom` (accurate but wraps to three lines at the
h3's clamp size, and duplicates the chip); `schools programmes` (general but characterless).

This keeps SEND/PRU/alternative provision as *part* of the reach rather than the whole definition —
the vault (§4) lists NEET and alternative provision alongside mainstream secondary, sixth form, FE
and youth organisations, ages 11–25.

## B · Prison arm — flexible vocational skills

The vault supports this reframe directly. Programme Library §3 describes the vocational course as
the programme and the family bike as *"the balance bike extension… the second strand"* — the site
currently inverts that, leading with the bond.

| Field | Now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| chip | `in prisons · after` | `in prisons · accredited` |
| name | `build to bond` | **`vocational skills`** |
| who | `for people inside prison rebuilding skills, family ties and a route to work on release.` | `for people inside prison earning a hands-on manufacturing qualification — six-week Build to Bond cohorts at Lowdham Grange, Foston Hall, Lindholme and Feltham.` |
| CTA | `commission a programme →` | `bring it to your prison →` (mirrors the destination page's own H2) |

**Proposed flow** — leads vocational, keeps the bond as one strand (peer point 7):
1. `six-week cohort :: six Makers, 18 hours a week`
2. `OCN Level 2 accredited :: workshop skills and sustainable manufacturing`
3. `a bike for their child :: handed over on family visit day`
4. `train as a peer instructor :: teach the next cohort`
5. `a route to work on release :: employer partners`

**The brand is kept, the positioning moves.** "Build to Bond" survives inside the `who` line rather
than as the pathway title — so the Minister's quote, the FT feature and the Inside Time article all
still land on a name the site uses, while the panel now leads with the vocational qualification.

**Flexibility is shown, not asserted** (peer point 6): naming the four active sites — Lowdham
Grange, Foston Hall, Lindholme and Feltham — is what Clink, Bounce Back and Redemption Roasters all
do. Naming the prisons BBC works at has been permitted since James's 2026-06-17 change; the
still-standing rule is never to link a *named participant* to a named site, which this copy does not.

**Where the "flexible" comes from** (all Proof Bank / Programme Library verified): six-week cohorts
of six · 18 hrs/week, 120 GLH · approved Cat B, C **and** D · hand tools only, numbered toolboxes,
full HMPPS security assessment · peer-instructor progression (3 trained at Lowdham Grange) ·
Train the Trainer route (§6) so a site can eventually run it with its own staff.

### ⚠ Naming caution — "Build to Bond" is brand equity, not just a label
It is the name in the **Prisons Minister's quote** ("Innovative projects such as Build to Bond…"),
the **FT feature** (5 Sep 2025) and the **Inside Time** article. Dropping it estate-wide would
discard recognition BBC has earned.

Recommendation: **reposition without renaming the programme.** Use `vocational skills inside` as the
pathway label on the impact page, and keep "Build to Bond" as the named programme on
`/pages/build-to-bond` and in press. If James wants the whole positioning shifted, note that
`/pages/build-to-bond` is bond-led from its H1 down (*"a bike built inside. a bond that goes home."*
· *"the bike is for their child. that's the point."*) — that is a separate, larger job, not covered
here.

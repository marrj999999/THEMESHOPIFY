Impact Page Stakeholder Audit

The impact page and the pages it hands people to, read the way each stakeholder reads them. The information each one needs comes from what they have actually asked Bamboo Bicycle Club for, in the vault's meeting notes, site notes, grant files and twelve years of archived email. Each need is checked against the live site and the v2 preview, and every gap is gated against the Claims Register.

Verdict

The impact page does its job for every stakeholder: it proves the work is real, dated and honestly counted, and it hands each reader to the right next page. The gaps are on those next pages and behind them. Commissioners get costs and routes but not the paperwork list their security and finance teams will ask for. Teachers get the free start but not what arrives for a class. Funders get the numbers but will find the documents they request do not yet exist. Four pages carry a claim the Register bans. And the single biggest loss across every audience is not a missing paragraph: it is enquiries that were never answered.

How this was read

  • The stakeholders are the eight the vault already walks in its 27 August alignment report: prison commissioner, council or youth commissioner, teacher or D&T lead, university partner, funder or grant-maker, press or policy researcher, supporter or donor (with corporate CSR buyers), and kit customer.
  • Their needs were taken from evidence of what they asked, not from what we assume they want: site notes for Lindholme, Northumberland, Foston Hall and Feltham; the HMPPS Wales brief; Camden and Waltham Forest meetings; the education deal history mined from twelve years of email; the Investec, National Lottery and trust files; the press log; and Parliamentary correspondence.
  • Coverage was checked on the live site today and on the v2 preview (theme 199089193334): the impact page, the prisons, build-to-bond, support-mission, programmes, impact-report and theory-of-change pages.
  • Every gap is marked with what it takes to close: a wording that is already approved, a decision from James, or evidence that does not yet exist.

What no page can say yet, because the document does not exist

Stakeholders ask for these by name. The site is right to stay silent, and the vault should treat each as a dated task.

  • An adopted safeguarding policy (two drafts, none adopted). Asked by funders, councils and HMPPS Wales.
  • The CIC's governing document and first accounts (accounts due 17 November 2026). Blocks Triangle Trust, Paul Hamlyn, Esmée Fairbairn and the Lottery's director test.
  • A signed COSHH assessment for the resin at each site. Lindholme's safe system of work requires it; the pack is a draft.
  • DBS or clearance evidence per named person, and insurance certificates with indemnity levels and product liability.
  • HMPPS's actual due-diligence questionnaire. Never confirmed; the Wales prep says "ask, don't assume".
  • A per-cohort outcomes scorecard (starts, completions, OCN results, withdrawals, feedback). Commissioners will be promised it; the growth plan defines it; no site reports it yet.

Recommendations, in order

  1. Ship the four claim fixes
    Three are prepared; the fourth is one word. They sit on the pages commissioners, teachers and journalists land on from impact.
  2. Answer before you add
    Every audience's biggest recorded loss is an unanswered enquiry. Point the site's contact promise at the shared inbox with cover named, and make the promise true. This outranks any new band.
  3. A one-page commissioner sheet on the prisons page
    Costs split capital and per-learner; course IDs and the no-QAN note; what BBC brings to the security team; what each cohort reports; who you contract with; when to buy. Durham's governor asked for exactly this. Every line is already approved or a process fact.
  4. "What arrives for a class" on every school tier
    The kit pages got the labelled diagram today; the school packs need the same list. The refund on record came from its absence.
  5. Three honest lines in "how we count"
    No employment tracking after release yet; no life-cycle assessment yet; the CIC's first accounts due November. Each one pre-empts a question a diligent reader will ask.
  6. Decisions only James can make
    School from-prices; a corporate price card and group-size statement; the commissioner reporting promise; the "this quarter" figures; the cohort wording; the Timpson attribution. Six decisions, each one line of copy.
  7. Build the documents
    Adopt the safeguarding policy, file the CIC accounts, sign the COSHH pack, confirm HMPPS's due-diligence list, start the per-cohort scorecard. These unlock more funders and sites than any page change.

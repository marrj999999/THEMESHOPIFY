# Synthesis

## (a) Common anatomy — what 7+ of 10 strong bike/kit PDPs do
1. Gallery left / buy box right, title + price + variant pickers + one CTA above the fold (10/10).
2. A stock or lead-time statement at the buy box: "In stock – dispatched in 2 days", "Delivery in 18–22 business days", "ETA Late Feb 2027", "Ships within 5 business days" (8/10; Surly/Specialized weakest).
3. A 3–6 item trust/reassurance strip near price: warranty length, return window, shipping promise (9/10 — Brompton ribbon, Ribble "Why Ribble", Canyon "Buying reasons", Cowboy tiles, Temple accordions, Framework ship/warranty line).
4. Financing/BNPL + Cycle-to-Work called out by name (Klarna, Shop Pay in 3, Cyclescheme) (7/10; not Surly/Specialized/My Boo).
5. Geometry table with a rider-height row or height-band graphic (8/10 — Canyon, Ribble, Surly, Temple, Specialized, Fairlight, My Boo, Brompton G Line).
6. Compatibility spec list for frames: BB, headset, seatpost dia/clamp, axle spacing, brake mount, max tyre (with/without guards), mounts (7/10 — Surly, Fairlight, Ribble, Temple, Canyon, Specialized partly, My Boo weak).
7. Feature story band(s) below the buy box, then spec, then geometry, then FAQ/documents, then cross-sell (9/10 follow roughly that order).
8. Downloadable documents / manuals (7/10 — Canyon PDF geo, Surly docs, Specialized manuals, Fairlight design notes + manual, Ribble help centre, Framework guides, Brompton support).

## (b) What only the best do
- Landed-cost transparency: Canyon "Approx. total incl. VAT + duty + shipping"; Fairlight tax/duty/shipping calculator + per-region duty pages; Ribble "All Tax and Duties Included".
- Interactive sizing: Canyon 3-step body-measure quiz; Fairlight fit calculator + R/T proportional sizes; Specialized "What's my size?".
- Assembly honesty as a spec: Framework "Setup 10–20 min · Difficulty Moderate"; Canyon "95% built, tools included, app walks you through"; Fairlight "how will my frameset arrive" (threads chased, BB faced); Ribble FAQ with torques and grease.
- Real-build galleries as social proof: Fairlight 13 customer builds (15–31 photos each); Temple custom-build gallery + reviews that are build stories.
- Deposit / refundable pre-order to hold a slot: Fairlight £750 refundable deposit; Framework cancel-anytime pre-order.
- Explicit limits: Fairlight rider 115 kg / rack 25 kg / fork 3 kg per leg; Canyon 120 kg; My Boo system weight 120 kg.
- "Complete the build" cross-sell for framesets: Specialized "Complete Your Ride"; Fairlight headset/dynamo/cage tiers inline; Framework "most popular combination".
- Human help CTA next to buy: Cowboy "Chat with an expert" / quiz; My Boo "request advice / request quote"; Canyon chat.
- Press quotes on the product page: Ribble (3), Fairlight (8), Cowboy (TIME, Red Dot).

## (c) What a frame-KIT page needs that a complete-bike page doesn't
1. "What's in the box" list, itemised (Temple "supplied with headset and seat bolt"; Ribble "headset, top cap, axles included"; Framework "bring your own" per component). Kit pages must also say what is NOT included (Fairlight: "we cannot ship components with a frameset"; Canyon "Pedals: none included").
2. Build effort, tools, time, skill level as spec rows (Framework), plus the step-by-step guide linked from the buy box (guides.frame.work; Canyon app; Temple assembly guide).
3. Compatibility standards so the buyer can source the rest: BB shell, headset SHIS, seatpost, spacing, brake mount, max tyre, max chainring, dropper yes/no (Surly is the template).
4. A "complete the build" module or a parts-list PDF (Specialized, Fairlight base-build table, Framework popular combo).
5. Sizing without a test ride: height→size in the option label (My Boo), geometry table with height row (Surly/Canyon), fit calculator (Fairlight).
6. Weight / rider limits and a standard the frame is tested to (Surly ASTM 2 sheet; Fairlight limits; Specialized weight-limit PDF) — more important for an unconventional material.
7. Provenance/how-it's-made band (Fairlight craftsmanship, Brompton factory, Cowboy "assembled in France"; My Boo leaves this OFF the PDP — a gap).
8. Lead time + refundable deposit (Fairlight) because kits are often made-to-order.
9. Shipping/duties for a box that is not a bike: frame-specific shipping price (Fairlight "Frames £30 UK"; Surly "free shipping excludes frames") and duty guidance for worldwide buyers.

## (d) Adopt / adapt / avoid for BBC
ADOPT
- Trust ribbon of 3 items site-wide (Brompton): e.g. "Lifetime bamboo frame guarantee · 30-day returns · Ships worldwide".
- Stock/lead-time line with checkmark at the buy box (Brompton "✓ In stock – dispatched within 2 working days"; Temple "Delivery in 18–22 business days").
- "Setup time · Difficulty · Tools included" spec rows (Framework) — the single most relevant borrow for a build-it-yourself kit.
- Frame Specs interface list (Surly) + tyre clearance with/without guards (Fairlight).
- Height range inside the size option label (My Boo) plus a geometry table with a rider-height row (Canyon/Surly).
- "Complete Your Ride" cross-sell block (Specialized) → BBC "Finish your build" (headset, BB, wheels, groupset bundle, tools).
- Landed-cost / duties line for worldwide buyers (Ribble "tax and duties included" or Canyon "approx. total" style, Fairlight duty pages).
- Product reviews that show the customer's build (Temple) and a real-builds gallery (Fairlight).
- Explicit rider/luggage weight limits and the test standard (Fairlight/Surly) — pre-empts the "is bamboo strong enough?" objection.
- Per-section FAQ links inside the configurator (Framework) and an assembly-oriented FAQ (Ribble: torques, grease, bearing sizes).

ADAPT
- Canyon's 3-step size quiz → a 2-question height/inseam picker is enough at BBC's scale.
- Fairlight's refundable deposit → useful only if BBC kits are made-to-order with a lead time; otherwise skip.
- Cowboy's "Which is right for you? Take the quiz" + compare table → a compare row for BBC's kit tiers (£375 vs £795: what changes).
- Ribble's "Save £x vs complete bike" badge → BBC: "vs a finished bamboo bike / vs a workshop course".
- Fairlight's separate explore vs buy pages → BBC can keep one page but use a sticky sub-nav (Cowboy/Framework) to Overview / What's in the box / Build / Spec / Reviews.
- Framework's "bring your own" options → BBC "I have my own headset/BB" toggles that remove items from the kit price.
- Cowboy's unfiltered reviews → show some critical reviews; credibility for an unusual material.

AVOID

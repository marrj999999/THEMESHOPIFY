Impact Page Benchmark

The Bamboo Bicycle Club impact page (live today, and the v2 build on the preview theme) scored against ten impact pages from comparable organisations, fetched live today: prison-rehabilitation social enterprises and bike charities. Every row says what the peers list, what we list, and what is left, gated against the vault's Claims Register.

Verdict

The page already covers most of what the peer set lists, and it is more honest than most of them about what it can prove. The real gaps were small and cheap: four approved facts that were never put on the page, a recognition row, a line saying who we are legally, and a link to the theory of change that already exists. All of those are now on the preview. What we cannot match is the peers' headline device, a reoffending or employment rate against a national baseline, and the vault says why. The page now says why too.

What went on the preview today

Six additions, every one an approved wording from the Claims Register or Proof Bank. Nothing on the live site changed.

  • 3,500+ bikes built in the record, in place of the "2 OCN London Level 2 courses" stat, which stays in the prisons strand card.
  • 108 contact hours per Maker, per course as the note on the hands-on-training station.
  • "We train the prison staff who run it: 13 trained as instructors in 2026, across three sites" in the prisons strand lede.
  • Recognition row under the honesty note: Investec Beyond Business 2025 winner, one of four from 148 applicants; Kevin McCloud's Green Heroes, Grand Designs Live 2018; Match: Design & Sport, Musée du Luxembourg, Paris 2024; National Justice Museum, Working Through It exhibition, 2026.
  • Identity line: Bamboo Mobility Project CIC, a community interest company, not a registered charity; registered UK learning provider, UKPRN 10098630; two OCN London bespoke Level 2 courses; activity since 2012, CIC incorporated 2025; workshops in London, Amersfoort, Toulouse and Brighton.
  • "Read the full theory of change" link under the stations, to the page that already existed but was linked from nowhere.

Verified

Gate passed (no raw values in the CSS, claim-lint clean), every pushed file read back identical, live-check on the preview shows zero banned claims and zero contradictions, screenshots at 1440 and 390 in the evidence folder.

What needs James before it can go on

  1. A "this quarter" strip, the Redemption Roasters device
    Q2 2026 figures from the Investec report: 31 learners engaged across prison programmes, 9 prison staff and peer mentors trained, 44 undergraduates reached across two universities. Dated, verifiable, and the vault already marks them as candidates. Needs a Register row and your sign-off.
  2. The n and year behind the 26% confidence uplift
    Four peers put a sample size on their survey stats. The Register approves the figure "with attribution"; adding "n = … , cohorts … to …" needs the evaluation source opened.
  3. The OCN external quality-assurance visit as an external check
    January 2026, seven Lowdham learners, run 11434643. The Register allows "delivered Level 2 assessment quality" and forbids implying Assured Status. Your call whether that boundary reads well on a public page.
  4. Cohort wording (decision D2)
    Switchback and Redemption say how many people a cohort holds. Recommended "cohorts of up to 8" is still pending.

Observations from the set

  • Peers are shorter. Redemption's page is about 3,500 characters of text; ours is about 15,000, mostly the 37 case-study cards. The evidence wall is a strength, but 12 cards plus "show all" would let the record, the stations and the honesty note carry more weight.
  • Peers put comparators on almost every number. That is the single biggest gap, and it is an evidence gap, not a page gap. The Justice Data Lab route is how a small organisation gets one without funding a study.
  • Most peers do not say how they count. Only World Bicycle Relief and Greenhouse do. The honesty note is a differentiator; keep it.
  • Half the peer set lists awards; none of ours were on the page. Four approved recognitions now are.
  • Nobody in the set names participants in connection with a prison. Redemption names the prisons its academies sit in but not the people; our rule is consistent with the field.
  • The Q2 report's tone is the model. "The numbers are modest and we have not manufactured any" is more credible than any peer's SROI figure, and it is already the voice of the honesty note.

WHY BAMBOO?
the material you can build — and understand — by hand
Grown on six continents. Shaped with hand tools. Tested in university labs. Bamboo is the frame material you can actually understand — and understanding is what makes it sustainable.

the science
a ride you can feel
Bamboo's fibres turn road buzz into a trace of heat — so less of the rough road ever reaches your hands.
A bamboo frame significantly improved ride comfort over an equivalent aluminium one.
peer-reviewedThite et al., Vehicle System Dynamics (2013) · Oxford Brookes University

03
strength & ride
strong where it counts
Tensile strength comparable to mild steel — tested to BS ISO 22157 at Swansea — with flax-and-carbon joints. It gives over the bumps and stays stiff under power, so it moves with you.
Independent engineers fatigue-modelled a bamboo frame to outlast around 26 times its intended service life.
peer-reviewedArango Fierro et al. (2019) · structural & fatigue analysis

THE EVIDENCE
the research behind bamboo
We don't just build with bamboo — we study it, and we teach it. A decade of testing, student projects and peer-reviewed research.
Swansea University — our bamboo tested to BS ISO 22157 (tensile ≈ 84 MPa). University College London — the "Pedal Progress" cargo-bike project, winner of the IMechE Group Project Award. University of Manchester — bamboo-composite materials research. Oxford Brookes University — peer-reviewed ride-comfort study. London South Bank University — campus innovation hub and composites R&D. Coventry University — UK Bamboo Summit 2024. University of Cumbria — Kate Rawles' 8,288-mile "Woody".
Key research: Thite et al., Vehicle System Dynamics (2013) · Wegst & Ashby, Philosophical Magazine (2004) · Amiri et al. on flax composites, Proc. IMechE (2018) · Arango Fierro et al., structural & fatigue analysis (2019).

Ranges reflect species, alloy and lay-up — bamboo especially varies with species, culm and moisture, which is exactly why we test to a standard. Our 84 MPa is a real culm section tested at Swansea (BS ISO 22157). † Carbon's headline strength is measured along the fibre; a frame tube only ever sees a fraction of it. Sources: Swansea University test data; Sharma & Harries (2015); Wegst & Ashby (2004); MatWeb / ASM.
Carbon is lighter and stiffer. Bamboo answers on damping and comfort — and it's the only one of the four you can build yourself, by hand, in a weekend.
PROVEN WITH SCIENCE
We don't ask you to take "bamboo is good" on faith. We test it, we teach it, and we work with universities to understand it better.
why this matters
understanding is the real sustainability*
the greenest material is the one you understand.
* understand what bamboo is, how it grows and how it's joined, and you use it better — you waste less, repair instead of replace, and keep improving. that's why everything we do is teaching.

DESIGN VERSATILITY
any design. any rider.
You can't shape steel in your kitchen or cut carbon in your garage. Bamboo you cut with a hand saw and join with wrapping and epoxy — so you can build any frame you can draw.

IN PRISONS · BUILD TO BOND
self-delivery: we set up, we train, you run it.
Built for prison industries and education teams. We install the workshop, train your staff to deliver the course, and support your first cohorts — then the capability stays in-house, with no ongoing fees.
scope
A site visit with your industries or education team: workshop space, security and tool-control requirements, cohort size.
set up
Jigs, tooling and materials installed — a one-time setup, typically £7–10k depending on your workshop.
train your team
We train your instructors and workshop staff to deliver the course themselves — the capability stays in-house, with no ongoing fees.
deliver & accredit
Repeatable six-week cohorts, budgeted around £8,500 each including materials and OCN Level 2 accreditation.
family visit day
Each cohort ends with Makers presenting the balance bikes they built to their own children — the bike goes home.

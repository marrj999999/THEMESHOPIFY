Home Frame Build Kits
Everything you need to build a bamboo frame at your own bench — bamboo, flax and bio-resin, machined metalwork, an illustrated manual and 15+ hours of video. If you can use a few hand tools, you can build this. Every kit funds accredited skills courses inside prisons and schools.

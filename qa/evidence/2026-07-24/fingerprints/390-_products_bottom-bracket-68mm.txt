Details & specs
A BSA English-threaded bottom bracket shell for bonding into a bamboo frame. The drive side is marked with a groove so it goes in the right way round.
Fits: all major groupsets, including Shimano, SRAM and Campagnolo. Available in 68mm and 73mm widths.
Type: BSA English threaded
Widths: 68mm and 73mm
Outside diameter: 40mm
Tolerance: +0.25mm (.010") to -0.5mm (.020")
Finish: knurled to aid bonding
Drive side: marked with a groove
Made in: UK
Compatibility
Works with standard frame builds. Contact us if unsure about compatibility with your specific build.

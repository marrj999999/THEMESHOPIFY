Build to Bond
OUR PRISON PROGRAMME
a bike built inside. a bond that goes home.
Build to Bond is a six-week bamboo bike-building course inside prison workshops. Makers learn real manufacturing skills, earn a recognised qualification — and finish by giving the bike they built to their own child on family visit day.

WITH HMPPS
INSIDE THE WORKSHOP
real manufacturing, taught by hand.
Inside a working prison workshop, small cohorts of Makers measure, mitre and bond bamboo frames by hand — the same craft we've taught in our London workshop since 2012. Sessions build real skills: precision marking-out, jig setup, structural bonding, finishing and quality checks.
Along the way, each Maker builds a portfolio of work and earns an OCN Level 2 in Workshop Skills and Sustainable Manufacturing — a recognised qualification, earned hands-on.
WHY IT WORKS
the bike is for their child. that's the point.
The finished build is a child's balance bike. On family visit day, the Maker hands it over themselves — the first thing many have ever made, given to the person who matters most.
Ministry of Justice research shows that people in prison who maintain family ties are 39% less likely to reoffend (Lord Farmer Review, 2017). And with just 20% of prison-leavers in work six weeks after release nationally (MoJ, 2025), the skills matter as much as the bond. Build to Bond is built around that evidence: a six-week course where the skills are real, the qualification is recognised, and the finish line is a stronger family bond.
The bike was the first thing I ever made with my own hands. When my daughter rode it on family visit day, I felt like a dad again.
— A BUILD TO BOND MAKER
HOW IT WORKS
six weeks, start to handover.
enrol
Makers join through the prison's workshops or education team. No prior experience needed.
build
Six weekly sessions in the workshop: frame layout, mitring, structural bonding and finishing — real manufacturing, taught by hand.
accredit
Portfolio work towards an OCN Level 2 in Workshop Skills and Sustainable Manufacturing — a recognised qualification.
family visit day
The finished balance bike is presented by the Maker to their own child — and goes home with them.
Watching the pride on a father's face when he presents a bike he built himself to his child — that is what rehabilitation looks like.
— SALLY ALLSOPP, INDUSTRIES MANAGER

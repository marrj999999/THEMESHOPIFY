FIND YOUR FIT
built to fit you
Bamboo Bicycle Club frame size guide — by height and inseam
FRAME SIZE	RIDER HEIGHT	INSEAM
XS	148–163 cm	68–74 cm
S	160–173 cm	74–80 cm
M	170–183 cm	78–86 cm
L	180–193 cm	84–92 cm
XL	190–203 cm	90–100 cm
2XL	200 cm +	98 cm +
Inseam is the better guide — measure barefoot, floor to crotch. Bands overlap on purpose: if you fall between two, choose the smaller frame.
Your frame's size is set on the jig you build on — so pick the size that matches your fit, then fine-tune with seatpost, stem and saddle.
If you're between sizes, build the smaller one. Outside the range, go custom.

HOW YOU'LL BUILD IT
four stages, across a few weekends.
01
set the geometry
Lock gravel geometry on the jig — wider clearance and a slacker head angle than road.
02
join & wrap
Bond the tubes with epoxy and wrap each joint with carbon-fibre tow.
03
cure
Leave it for a full cure — that's where maximum joint strength comes from.
04
sand, seal & ride
Sand smooth, seal the bamboo, fit headset and BB. Adventure awaits.
WHY BAMBOO
a smoother, more compliant ride.
Bamboo's natural vibration damping takes the edge off rough trails — ideal for bikepacking. Frames are compatible with all major groupsets and can be built around Shimano GRX or Rohloff geared hubs.
The current trend is for carbon frames that lack environmental credentials. Bamboo is easy to grow, offsets its carbon footprint, and — combined with flax and bio-composites — offers a viable alternative.
in the kit
Heat-treated Moso bamboo tubes (with spares), a full-size frame blueprint, a plywood alignment jig, the CNC metal parts that the steering, cranks and wheels bolt to (head tube, bottom-bracket shell and dropouts), carbon-fibre tow (the reinforcing fibre you wrap each joint with), two-part epoxy resin, and an illustrated manual plus 15+ hours of step-by-step video tutorials.
you'll add
Fork, wheels, groupset, handlebars, saddle and finishing components. Grab a complete component pack from us, or source parts from any bike shop.
BUILD WITH CONFIDENCE
you've got this — and we've got you
you can't really ruin it
The glue is slow and adjustable, each joint wrap is additive (more = stronger), and a cured joint simply sands back to shape. The kit even ships with spare bamboo — at no single point is a mistake permanent.

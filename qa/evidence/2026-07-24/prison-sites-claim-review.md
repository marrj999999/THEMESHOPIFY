## Why this matters

"Live at … HMYOI Feltham" asserts delivery. On the vault record, Feltham has never delivered a
cohort and has been awaiting a response for over two months. Foston Hall has capability but no
running cohort. The defensible reading of the evidence is **two sites delivering, one trained and
paused, one in approval** — not four live.

This is the same class of risk as the LSBU "£70,000 equipment partnership" found earlier today:
a claim about a named third party (here, HMPPS establishments and Shaw Trust) that the evidence
on file does not carry.

## Why I did not change it

1. **CLAUDE.md explicitly forbids the alternative** ("never 'three prisons'"), so silently
   rewriting it would override a standing instruction of James's.
2. **The vault may lag reality.** James has direct knowledge; Feltham or Foston Hall may have
   started since the notes were last updated (Feltham's note: 13 July; Foston: 20 July).
3. The **Claims Register has no ruling on site count** — this has never been adjudicated, so
   there is no canonical wording to apply.

## What James needs to decide

- If all four are genuinely delivering → **update the vault notes**, and ideally add a site-count
  row to the Claims Register so the gates can hold the line in future.
- If not → the wording needs to distinguish delivery from pipeline. Something like

  Feltham in approval"* is accurate without understating the footprint, and avoids the banned
  "three prisons" framing entirely by not counting at all.

inside the workshop

real manufacturing, taught by hand.

Inside a working prison workshop, small cohorts of Makers measure, mitre and bond bamboo frames by hand. The same craft we have taught in our London workshop since 2012. Sessions build real skills: precision marking-out, jig setup, structural bonding, finishing and quality checks.

Along the way, each Maker builds a portfolio of work towards OCN London's bespoke Level 2, Workshop Skills and Sustainable Manufacturing, earned hands-on.

why it works

the bike is for their child. that's the point.

The finished build is a child's balance bike. On family visit day, the Maker hands it over themselves: the first thing many have ever made, given to the person who matters most.

Ministry of Justice research found prisoners who receive family visits are 39% less likely to reoffend. With just 20% of prison-leavers in work six weeks after release nationally, the skills matter as much as the bond. Build to Bond is built around that evidence: a six-week course where the skills are real, the qualification is assessed, and the finish line is a stronger family bond.

how it works

six weeks, start to handover.

One cohort, six weeks, eighteen hours a week. Every Maker builds a full bamboo frame and finishes with a bike that goes home.

enrol

Makers join through the prison's workshops or education team. No prior experience needed.

week 6 · portfolio assessed

family visit day

The finished balance bike is presented by the Maker to their own child, and goes home with them.

the programme

two courses, one progression.

course one · six weeks

Workshop Skills and Sustainable Manufacturing

OCN London bespoke Level 2, centre course 1130735. Measuring, mitring, bonding and finishing a full bamboo frame, with a portfolio of evidence. 108 contact hours per Maker.

course two · the bike that goes home

Introduction to Product Design & Manufacture (Bamboo Balance Bike)

OCN London bespoke Level 2, centre course 1131207. Makers design and build a child's balance bike and present it on family visit day.

inside the workshop

real manufacturing, taught by hand.

Inside a working prison workshop, small cohorts of Makers measure, mitre and bond bamboo frames by hand. Each Maker builds a portfolio towards OCN London's bespoke Level 2.

watch the film · 3 min · subtitled

Filmed inside HMP Lowdham Grange by the prison's Inside Media Department. Subtitled. Makers are not identifiable.

why it works

the bike is for their child. that's the point.

On family visit day, the Maker hands the balance bike over themselves. Ministry of Justice research found prisoners who receive family visits are 39% less likely to reoffend.

how it works

six weeks, start to handover.

enrol

Makers join through the prison's workshops or education team. No prior experience needed.

weeks 0 to 1 · induction and first craft

build

Frame layout, mitring, structural bonding and finishing. Real manufacturing, taught by hand.

weeks 1 to 5 · the frame

accredit

Portfolio work towards OCN London's bespoke Level 2, Workshop Skills and Sustainable Manufacturing.

week 6 · portfolio assessed

family visit day

The finished balance bike is presented by the Maker to their own child, and goes home with them.

the last day · the bike goes home

Introduction to Product Design & Manufacture (Bamboo Balance Bike)

OCN London bespoke Level 2, centre course 1131207. A child's balance bike, presented on family visit day.

what we do and don't claim

the tubing is tested. your joints decide the frame.

Bamboo frame tubing tested at Swansea University to BS ISO 22157:2019 — 84.3 MPa ultimate tensile, comparable to mild steel at a fraction of the weight. That figure is for the tubing we supply, not for a finished frame: how strong your bike ends up is decided by the joints you make. Follow the manual, let the resin cure fully, and check the joints before every early ride — and call us if anything looks wrong.

before you build

the questions the emails ask.

Ranked by how often builders ask them, not by how we'd like to be asked.

Do I need power tools?

No. Just basic hand tools — a hacksaw, files, sandpaper, a measuring tape and a work surface around 1200 × 600 mm. Everything else is in the kit.

What drivetrain and groupset can I run?

The frame works with all major road groupsets. Add one of our matched component packs, or source your own parts from any bike shop.

Is it weatherproof?

Yes. Finished frames are sealed with epoxy resin. Dry your bike after wet rides and store it indoors.

Not sure which size?

Send us your height and inside-leg measurement and we'll recommend the fit — or choose a Custom frame, made to your measurements.

Can I use Cycle to Work?

Yes — build your bamboo bike through the government's Cycle to Work scheme and save through salary sacrifice.

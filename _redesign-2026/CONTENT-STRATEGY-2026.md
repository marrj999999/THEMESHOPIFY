# BBC Content Strategy — research-backed, 2026-06-18

Built from: 21 best-practice principles + audits of Patagonia, Rapha, Brompton, Brooks England, Finisterre, Peak Design, Who Gives A Crap, Toast Ale, Elvis & Kresse, Temple Cycles.

## The one-line strategy
**Turn ~460 buried blog posts into a named, browsable proof engine that runs on two tracks — Builder stories (product) and Second-Chance stories (mission) — each carrying a name, a place, one hard number, and a first-person quote, surfaced at every decision point and wired back to a kit.**

## The five moves every exemplar shares (our north star)
1. **A named Stories HUB with cause-based entry points** (Patagonia "Stories", Finisterre "Broadcast", Brompton "Stories", Brooks "Journal"). → Build a BBC hub tagged **Builders · Planet · Second Chances · Workshops · Education**.
2. **A rigid story template** (Brompton): *Name · place · what they do · one specific number/route · one line on why.* No vanity quotes, always a face + name (principles 16–17).
3. **Featured-stories band in the conversion path + rotating "Editors' Picks"** (Finisterre, principle 13) — the blog currently has **no homepage entry point**; this is the single biggest gap.
4. **Two-way internal linking**: each story → its kit; each kit page → 1–3 matching stories near add-to-cart (principles 11–12).
5. **Mission = emotional story + hard number, costed** (Toast, WGAC, Elvis & Kresse): translate £11.41/£1, 4,000+ builders into human-scale, costed outcomes, near the buy button.

## BBC's unfair advantages (use them — competitors can't copy)
- **The build is lived, not bought** → Brooks-style "How it's made / Product Portraits" of the frame build = our teaching *is* craft content.
- **Real human transformation (prison rehab)** → more powerful than anything on those 10 sites — *if* the safeguarding line holds: **name a person OR a prison, never a named participant tied to a named site.** Tell process + impact + numbers; anonymise identities where needed (Brooks' "name the craft, not the person" is the correct mode here).
- **A decade-proven, self-repairable frame** → Patagonia Worn-Wear "longevity/repair" customer-as-hero strand (Taro: 10 yrs, 180km Dunwich Dynamo).

## Roadmap (prioritised)
**P1 — surface what we already have (this is mostly free; the stories exist + all 27 links are healthy):**
- [BUILD] **Homepage "Latest from the Club" stories band** beside the conversion path — 3–4 hand-picked, rotating (10,000km journey, a second-chance story, Taro, a corporate build).
- [BUILD] **Impact credibility band** — the FT/Lord Timpson quote + costed social value.
- [BUILD] **10,000km flagship** added to Why Bamboo builds (or Ride-quality quote).

**P2 — structure:**
- A **"Where our bikes go" / Builders hub** pillar page (Brompton/Finisterre model), tagged, with Editors' Picks carousel.
- **1–3 matching stories on each kit page** near add-to-cart (peer-level proof, principle 7/10).
- A **costed social-value line + counter** near buy ("every kit helps fund a build-to-bond place").

**P3 — engine:**
- A **named "Builders / Pathfinders" series** (Temple/Brompton) — portrait + place + their frame + their ride log; graduates where consent allows.
- A **branded hashtag + on-site UGC submit** (#BambooBicycleClub) — self-replenishing community content.
- An **annual BBC Impact Report** (Toast) — re-tells the rehab + build story with fresh numbers; a yearly press hook.

## Copy rules (apply everywhere)
- Lead with the dramatic line/raw quote, never background (principle 3).
- Every story: name + place + one hard number + first-person quote.
- Pair each page claim with matching proof ("no experience needed" → a beginner's quote).
- Kill "amazing, highly recommend." Specifics only.
- Impact: dignity over pity; agent-of-their-own-change framing.

## 3. Best-practice rebuild plan (mirror the strong Education page)
1. **Hero** — "Your team builds a real bamboo bike together — and gives it away." + one honest proof number + single "Request a quote" CTA + a real action photo.
2. **Client logo strip** — restrained, first scroll: "Teams from these brands have built with us." (Four Seasons · GM · Macallan · Hilton). The page's signature asset.
3. **Key facts box** (stamp-card stamps) — group size · 3 hrs–full day · your venue UK-wide or ours · indoor/outdoor · no experience needed · bikes can be donated.
4. **What the day actually is** — concrete craft verbs (cut, mitre, bind, lacquer); "not a flat-pack kit — your team makes a frame from raw bamboo."
5. **The human / charity story** — donate-the-bike loop + the social-enterprise CIC moat (the fee funds BBC's social work). Raw bamboo → built → donated → ridden.
6. **How it works** — 4–6 stamped steps, a real photo each.
7. **What's included** — facilitation, all materials, insurance + risk assessment, travel, charity handover, + a CSR/ESG impact summary for the team's report (a deliverable few competitors offer).
8. **Case studies** — 3–4 cards (logo + 2 sentences + a one-line quote): Four Seasons, GM, Macallan, Upcycle Brixton.
9. **Proof / outcomes** — "what your team gets" vs "what the community gets".
10. **Credibility strip** — social-enterprise CIC (Bamboo Mobility Project CIC) · enhanced-DBS facilitators · public-liability insured · Investec Beyond Business 2025.
11. **Gallery** — real build/finished-bike photos.
12. **FAQ** — group sizes / where the bikes go & can we keep them / how physical / cost model (quote per group) / lead time.
13. **Final CTA band** — "Request a quote" + "a proper quote back within one working day".

**Voice:** plain, understated, British — a deliberate wedge against the "high-energy / unforgettable" category. Request-a-quote only, no public price.

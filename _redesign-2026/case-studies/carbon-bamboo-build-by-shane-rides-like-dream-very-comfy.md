# Shane Built a Bamboo Road Bike at Home — "Rides Like a Dream"

Shane built a bamboo road frame at home from one of our build kits and was delighted with the result — comfortable, easy to live with, and a bike he would happily recommend.

## What he did
Working from a home build kit, Shane mitred and bound the bamboo himself and finished the frame with his own choice of components. He hit one honest snag along the way — the rear triangle did not set right first time, so he took it apart and built it again, and the second attempt came out perfectly. He found fitting the components the straightforward part. His one suggestion to us: include a few spares in the kit, in case a step needs redoing.

## Verified outcome
A first-time builder produced a road frame he was happy to ride and recommend — and learned to fix his own mistake in the process. That is exactly what a home build kit is for.

## In his words
> "Rides like dream. Very comfy!"

## Why it matters
You do not need a workshop or any framebuilding background to build a bike you can trust. With the jigs, the instructions and a bit of patience, Shane built his own road frame at home and rode away happy. Since 2012, more than 4,000 people across 36 countries have built their own bamboo bikes the same way.

# road.cc built a bamboo bike with our kit

Back in 2018, the cycling site **road.cc** put one of our kits to the test the honest way: they had their own Dave — **Dave Atkinson** — build a bamboo road bike from scratch and film the whole thing.

It became a multi-part video series, running roughly weekly from **April 2018**, that took Dave from "building a bamboo bike" jig all the way through to a finished frame and his first ride out in the Mendips — with the stated goal of lining up for a 3rd-cat race. road.cc summed it up when the bike was done:

> "After much sanding and some swears, our Dave has finally got his bamboo bike up and running. And do you know what? it looks pretty cool."
> — road.cc, "Building a bamboo bike part 6: time for a ride!", 25 May 2018

That's exactly what we're after: a genuine first-timer, no shortcuts, building a real road bike from one of our home kits and riding it. No frame-building experience required — just a bit of patience and a weekend or two.

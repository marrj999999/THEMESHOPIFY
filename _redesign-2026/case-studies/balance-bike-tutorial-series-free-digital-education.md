## The idea

We believe learning to build a bamboo bicycle should be open to anyone, anywhere. So we put our
balance bike build online, free to watch, on our YouTube channel — a complete walkthrough that
takes you from raw bamboo to a finished frame a young child can ride.

## What we did

In November 2024 we published a four-part "How to Build a Bamboo Balance Bike" series on our
official YouTube channel. It follows the real Bamboo Bicycle Club home-build method, one stage at
a time, so you can build along at your own pace:

1. **[Part 1 — Cutting the Frame](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GevFK1s-I8w)**
2. **[Rear Triangle and Dropouts](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QKM6n6OGYAo)**
3. **[Part 3 — Gluing the Frame with Epoxy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ehu8Zn4IQ6s)**
4. **[Part 4 — Finishing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qgGjbDPQWHc)**

The whole series is free and globally accessible — no login, no fee, no specialist workshop
required to follow along.

## Verified outcome

- A genuine, complete four-part build series, published 15 November 2024 on our official channel.
- Free, open-access education in bamboo frame-building for anyone with an internet connection.
- Part of how Bamboo Bicycle Club shares its method openly: we have trained **4,000+ people across
  36+ countries since 2012**.

## In our words

> "The whole goal of Bamboo Bicycle Club is to make bike-building accessible to everyone."
> — James Marr

## Why it matters

A balance bike is the simplest frame we teach — which makes it the perfect way to learn the craft.
Putting the full build online for free means a teacher, a parent, or a young maker anywhere in the
world can follow the same steps we use in our workshops, and finish with a real, ridable bamboo
bike.

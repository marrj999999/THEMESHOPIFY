# Designboom features the Bamboo Urban Mini Velo

On 26 November 2020, the international design magazine **Designboom** featured a custom city bike built by Bamboo Bicycle Club in collaboration with the London architecture practice **mccloy + muchemwa**.

The "bamboo urban mini velo" is a small-wheeled bike with a split frame and a central cargo compartment sized to carry an A3 portfolio and a laptop — no basket, no backpack. The biocomposite, dual top-tube frame is made from bamboo, flax-fibre bindings and a plant-based resin, with the bindings sculpted into bone-like nodes and lights recessed into four small protruding stalks.

The brief came out of a competition run by the charity Article 25, and the finished bike was later nominated for The Index Project's Index Award.

Speaking to Designboom, architect Steve McCloy said:

> "There is freedom for creativity when working with bamboo because the tooling is low-tech, safe and accessible."

The frame was fabricated at the Bamboo Bicycle Club workshop, and the design is available as a home-build kit.

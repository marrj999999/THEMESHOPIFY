# Huck Magazine: making sustainable cycling accessible

Back in **2014**, Huck Magazine featured the Bamboo Bicycle Club as part of **The Working Artisans' Club** — a year-long series presented by Huck and O'Neill, celebrating European makers working at the intersection of craft, sustainability and community.

Photographed by **James Cannon** at our Hackney Wick workshop, the feature looked past the bikes to the idea behind them: that anyone who walks in can ride back out having built their own. As Huck put it:

> "Everyone that walks into their workshop rides back out with the knowledge and tools they need to make personalised bikes out of minimalist bamboo."

And on what drives the workshop model:

> "For Ian and James, it's not about shifting units but sharing the enjoyment and satisfaction that comes from pedalling along on your own handcrafted machine."

Founders **Ian McMillan** and **James Marr** built the club around knowledge-sharing rather than retail — charging for the experience of making, not for a finished product off a shelf. It's a philosophy that still defines everything we do.

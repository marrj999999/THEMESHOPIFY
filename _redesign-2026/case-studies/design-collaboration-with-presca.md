# Jerseys to match the bikes: our design collaboration with Presca

You spend two days building a bamboo frame with your own hands. It feels wrong to then pull on a jersey made the throwaway way. So we teamed up with Presca to make one that belongs on a bike you built yourself.

## The idea

Builders kept asking the same thing: once the frame's finished, what do we wear to ride it? We wanted an answer that matched the bike — made thoughtfully, from recycled materials, built to be kept and repaired rather than binned. Presca was the obvious partner.

## Who Presca are

Presca describes itself as the world's first climate-positive sportswear brand, with a mission to make the most environmentally conscious sportswear it can. Recycled materials, circularity, repair over replacement — the same values we build into a bamboo frame, applied to what you wear on top of it.

## What we made

Together we created a small run of exclusive cycling jerseys from recycled materials — designed with input from our building community rather than handed down from a brand. The plan is to keep it deliberately limited: refresh the design each year and open a short, roughly two-week order window before each batch goes into production, so we make what people actually want and little that's wasted.

## Why it matters

A bamboo frame is a statement about how things should be made. The jersey is the same statement, worn. Both are built to last, both are made from materials chosen with the planet in mind, and both come out of the same belief — that the things we use are better when someone has thought about how they're made.

## Want one?

We refresh the design each year and only open ordering for a short window. Register your interest and we'll let you know when the next batch is live.

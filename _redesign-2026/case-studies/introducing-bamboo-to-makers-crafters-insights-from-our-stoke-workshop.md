# Bamboo building comes to Stoke: a workshop with Brilliant Bamboo

> **Duplicate notice:** this post and *"Stoke-on-Trent College — bamboo workshop with students"* describe the **same March 2025 event**. The college post is the fuller, canonical write-up; recommend redirecting this URL to it. The verified copy below is what should run if this page is kept.

## The context

Most people who build with us come to our London workshop — but bamboo bicycle building was never meant to stay in one place. In early March 2025 we took a frame-building class north to Stoke-on-Trent, in partnership with **Brilliant Bamboo CIC**, a Staffordshire community interest company founded by Gemma Thomas. We know Brilliant Bamboo through the Bamboo in the UK group, an informal network of people working with the material across the country.

## What we did

Over two days, Brilliant Bamboo handled the local organising, the venue and the college links; we brought the bamboo, the bike-frame parts, the tools and the teaching.

On **Monday 3 March** we taught two groups of further-education students — around ten in each — the basics of working with bamboo as a structural material: how it is cut, shaped and joined, and how those properties shape what you can build with it.

On **Tuesday 4 March** we ran a public masterclass at the **Acava studio, Spode Works, Stoke-on-Trent**, for a smaller group of makers, crafters and creatives. The day moved from making small bamboo products in the morning to planning bike-frame geometry in the afternoon — then mitring, shaping and fitting bamboo tubes into prefabricated lugs, the same lugged frame-build method we teach in London.

The teaching follows the same hands-on approach behind our OCN London accreditation: learn by doing, with a real object taking shape on the bench in front of you.

## The outcome

We introduced a new group of students and makers in a new part of the country to building with bamboo, and showed that methods we have developed over more than a decade can travel — delivered alongside a strong local partner. Brilliant Bamboo had skilled tuition for its programme; we reached people who would never have travelled to London.

## Why it matters

We have trained more than 4,000 people across 36-plus countries since 2012, and none of that happens if the knowledge stays locked in one workshop. Stoke is a small, honest example of how it moves: a bench, a pile of canes, a college group and a local partner — and people finding out they can build something they never thought they could.

# Carbon Lugs: How We Made Bamboo Bike Building Accessible

## Context

A bamboo frame built the traditional way — wrapping and bonding every tube junction by hand — takes around **70 hours**. That is rewarding, but it is also a real barrier for a lot of people who want to build their own bike but cannot give up two working weeks to do it. So we set out to keep everything good about a hand-built bamboo frame while making it far quicker and more repeatable to assemble.

## What we did

We developed **pre-moulded carbon lugs** — the joints that hold the bamboo tubes together. Working with academics at **Oxford Brookes University** (with the University of West London), we 3D-printed the lugs in nylon-reinforced carbon fibre and bonded the frame with a high-strength urethane adhesive. Designing the lug around the frame let us build in the things a good frame needs from the start: stiffness for efficient power transfer, the correct tolerances for standard components, and tidy internal cable routing.

We proved the idea in the most public way we could. At the **Design Museum in London**, during the Cycle Revolution exhibition, we designed, 3D-printed, built and test-rode the **world's first 3D-printed bamboo bicycle — live, in front of visitors**.

## Verified outcome

- The pre-moulded carbon lug became BBC's **easy-build kit** — a real, shipping product.
- It cuts the frame build time from **around 70 hours to about 4 hours** — roughly a **75% reduction** — while keeping the bike genuinely buildable at home with basic tools.
- The world's-first 3D-printed bamboo bike was **built live in about 8 hours** at the Design Museum.

## Why it matters

As a society we need more local production, more low-carbon materials, and more products that can be built and repaired by the people who own them. The carbon lug is how we made a hand-built bamboo frame something far more people can actually take on — without losing the craft, the ride quality, or the bamboo's natural vibration damping.

> "By combining the lug with bamboo, it offers a natural vibration dampening and resolves the current bicycle market's lack of flexibility and high costs." — Designboom

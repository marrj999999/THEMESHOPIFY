# We filmed a whole bamboo cargo bike, one episode a week

**A new live build series, following a small bamboo cargo frame from raw tube to finished bike — episode by episode, nothing skipped.**

## The idea

Most build films jump to the satisfying bits. We wanted to do the opposite: show the whole
thing, slowly, in the order it actually happens. So we started a weekly series and pointed the
camera at a single project — a small bamboo cargo frame with a 20-inch front wheel, a relaxed
and fairly simple shape that still covers a lot of ground.

## What we did

We built the frame from scratch and released it one episode a week. The series walks through
the real stages of a bamboo build: designing the frame, setting up the jig, mitring the tubes,
the front triangle, the rear triangle, then all the binding and the finishing at the end. If
you are thinking about building your own, it is a good one to follow — it is the same process
we teach in the workshop, just filmed.

## Why it matters

Sharing the full method, not just the highlights, is the whole point of Bamboo Bicycle Club:
we would rather you understood how to make one than watch us make one. It is the same
knowledge-sharing approach that runs through our kits, our workshops and our education and
rehabilitation programmes.

In December 2020, UK cycling publication **[road.cc](https://road.cc/content/feature/bike-bedtime-bamboo-road-bike-easy-build-home-kit-279491)** featured our easy build home kit in its "Bike at bedtime" series.

The piece highlighted how the easy build kit slashes construction time from around **70 hours** with our original kit down to roughly **four hours** — a 75% reduction — thanks to pre-moulded carbon lugs that remove the need to create your own bamboo-to-bamboo connections.

## What road.cc covered

- **Easy build home kit: £395**, with pre-moulded carbon lugs.
- A frame featuring **32mm tyre clearance**, mudguard and pannier mounts, internal cabling and a disc brake frame adapter.
- Carbon lugs at around **0.7kg** and bamboo at **1kg–1.5kg**.
- A complete kit including a detailed build manual, full-scale drawing, alignment jig and high-strength bonding glue — built on a standard ~1200mm x 600mm table.

The feature noted bamboo's "excellent vibration dampening effects", resistance to stress and durability, and quoted our commitment: *"We are committed to enabling people to build cutting-edge bikes that are sustainable and can be completely recycled after many years of usage."*

Founded in London in 2012 by James Marr, Bamboo Bicycle Club helps people build their own bamboo bikes at home and in our workshops.

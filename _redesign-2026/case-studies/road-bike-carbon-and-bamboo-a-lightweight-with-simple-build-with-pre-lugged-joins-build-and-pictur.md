# The Road Carbon-Lug: a lightweight bamboo road frame, built fast

## Built by hand, in fewer hours

The Road Carbon-Lug is our lightest, most premium frame — and one of the quickest BBC
builds to make at home. Instead of wrapping every joint by hand, you bond your bamboo tubes
into **pre-formed carbon lugs**. The lugs do the precision work, so you spend less time on
the joins and more time riding.

It's the same idea the press picked up on: a real bamboo road frame you build yourself,
without the long, fiddly joint work the original method demanded.

## What it is

A carbon-and-bamboo road frame with pre-lugged joints, designed for drop-bar road riding.
It's the frame to choose if you want the comfort of bamboo with a faster, more repeatable
build than the traditional hemp-and-glue route.

- **Frame:** carbon-lugged bamboo road frame
- **Build method:** bond-into-lug (no joint-wrapping) — faster than hemp-and-glue
- **Best for:** riders who want a lightweight road frame and a quicker home build

Build it up with your choice of road groupset, wheels, drop bars and finishing kit — it's
your frame, your spec.

## Why bamboo, honestly

A BBC bamboo frame weighs **under two kilograms** and is **stronger by weight than steel in
tension**. Bamboo gives natural vibration damping that aluminium and carbon fibre can't
quite replicate — which is what makes a long road ride more comfortable.

On carbon: an independently verified **2024 lifecycle analysis** found a BBC bamboo frame
produces **56.7% less CO₂ than its aluminium equivalent** across the full production cycle.

We don't claim bamboo is stronger than steel in absolute terms, or quote test numbers we
can't stand behind. The case for bamboo doesn't need exaggeration.

## What real builders say

Carbon-lug builders tell us the lugs make the gluing easier and the alignment cleaner than
hand-wrapping. As one home builder put it after building his carbon-lug road frame:

> "The ride is forgiving — softer than my carbon and alloy bikes." — Ian, home builder, Kent

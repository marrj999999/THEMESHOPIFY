# BikeRadar rides a Bamboo Bicycle Club frame

On **29 January 2015**, the UK cycling site **BikeRadar** filmed a first ride of a Bamboo Bicycle Club frame. The feature was written by **Gregor MacGregor** and ridden on camera by BikeRadar's **Joe Norledge** — Bamboo Bicycle Club's first feature in the professional cycling press.

What BikeRadar liked was the honesty of a frame you build yourself. As MacGregor put it:

> "It would be churlish to try and measure a bike like this against a state of the art carbon machine."

> "Forget the tiny flaws and rough look to the build — that's a huge part of the charm."

And on how you get one:

> "To get one of these bamboo beauties you go on a two-day course to make your own (although home-build kits are now available too)."

That last line still holds: you can build your own at a two-day workshop, or at home from a kit.

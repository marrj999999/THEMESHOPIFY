# Jon took his self-built gravel bike into Epping Forest

Jon built a gravel bike on one of our flax-and-bamboo frames, then rode it where a
gravel bike belongs: out on the trails of Epping Forest, on London's doorstep.

## What he built

A bamboo frame reinforced with natural flax fibre — the same combination we supply in
our flax frame kits. Bamboo and flax are a good pairing for off-road riding: the natural
fibres help damp out trail buzz, so longer days on rougher ground stay comfortable.

## Why it matters

Every bike like Jon's starts the same way — a builder, a workshop or a kit at the kitchen
table, and a frame made by hand. The reward isn't just the finished bike. It's getting to
ride something you built yourself, somewhere worth riding.

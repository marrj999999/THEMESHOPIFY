In January 2023, the design publication **Designboom** featured Bamboo Bicycle Club's Bamboo Gravel Bike Build Kit, under the headline *"Bamboo Bicycle Club Lets You Build Your Own Sustainable Commute"* (edited by Ravail Khan).

Designboom set out how the kit lets people build a sustainable bamboo bike at home, opening:

> "Promoting local manufacturing, DIY, and independence, Bamboo Bicycle Club's Bamboo Gravel Bike Build Kit revolutionises our relationship with cycling." — Designboom

The piece described the kit's recycled-aluminium lugs joined to bamboo poles, giving handmade quality, natural vibration damping, an extended design life and recyclability. Using the pre-made lugs makes the build around **75% faster** than the traditional bamboo frame-building process, which can take up to 80 hours.

Read the original article on Designboom: [Bamboo Bicycle Club Lets You Build Your Own Sustainable Commute](https://www.designboom.com/design/bamboo-bicycle-allows-you-construct-your-own-sustainable-commute-james-marr-01-26-2023/) (26 January 2023).

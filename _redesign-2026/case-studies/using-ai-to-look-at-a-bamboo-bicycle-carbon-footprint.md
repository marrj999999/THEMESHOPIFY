# A sustainability specialist did the carbon maths on his own bamboo bike

## Context
Manish Godkhindi has spent 25-plus years in data and AI and more than a decade in sustainability work — net zero, ESG, climate. So when he built a bamboo frame with Bamboo Bicycle Club, he did what came naturally: he sat down and worked out its carbon footprint, then rode it from London to Brighton.

## What we did
Manish built his frame at BBC — bamboo tubes joined with natural-fibre lugs, not carbon fibre. He then trained for three months and rode the bike roughly seventy miles to the coast, a ride that took him about seven hours.

## Verified outcome
Manish's carbon comparison is his own back-of-an-envelope reasoning — he is careful to say it is not a formal lifecycle analysis. He pitched his bamboo frame against a Trek Madone, whose published figure puts the whole bike at about 192 kg of CO₂e (the carbon-fibre frame alone roughly 56 kg). Using bamboo's sequestration figures, he estimated his own small bamboo frame at a negative footprint of around 3.3 kg of CO₂ — carbon locked in as the grass grew rather than burned to make it. He calls the result "not a scientific calculation", and that honesty is the point: the direction of travel is obvious even before you reach for a lab.

Where BBC does have an independently verified figure, it is this: a 2024 lifecycle analysis found a BBC bamboo frame produces 56.7% less carbon than its aluminium equivalent across the full production cycle.

## In their words
> "In today's consumer driven mass produced society, little thought is given to the design, function and making process of the items we purchase." — James Marr, Luxembourg Museum Exhibition, 2023

## Why it matters
Bamboo grows fast — some species add a metre a day — and sequesters around 35% more carbon per acre than equivalent tree cover. A BBC frame weighs under two kilograms and, by weight, bamboo is stronger than steel in tension, with natural vibration damping that aluminium and carbon fibre cannot match. When someone who measures carbon for a living chooses to build and ride one, the material makes its own case.

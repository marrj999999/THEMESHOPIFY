# Five hand-built bamboo bikes, 1000 miles through Italy

Five riders. Five frames they built with their own hands. One thousand miles of Italian road.

## The ride

Over the summer of 2019, five hand-built bamboo bikes took on a thousand-mile journey through Italy, following the historic 1955 Mille Miglia route — starting and finishing in Brescia and passing through Rome, Florence and Bologna.

Every frame was built by its rider in our London workshop. The bikes carried different finishes and setups — some natural cane, some torched, with tyres and gearing chosen to suit each builder.

## What we did

We don't just build bikes in a workshop and send them off. Trips like this are where the frames earn their reputation. A thousand miles tests equipment, fitness and friendship at once — and doing it on a bike you built yourself adds pride when things work and responsibility when they don't.

## Why it matters

A long ride on a frame you made yourself is about as honest a test as bamboo gets. This wasn't a novelty outing; it was real-world proof that a hand-built bamboo frame is durable, capable touring equipment.

# BBC Case-Study Standard (what every one must contain)

**Purpose:** these are real stories about real people and partners. Truth over polish. Anything not verifiable from a primary source is removed, not guessed.

## Required structure
1. **Headline** — person/story-first, concrete. Never a number, price or bare noun-phrase. (e.g. *"He told us he wasn't good with his hands."* not *"Bamboo Bike Building Case Study"*.)
2. **Standfirst** (1–2 sentences) — who + the hook.
3. **Context / the challenge** (1 para) — who they are, the situation, why it mattered.
4. **What we did** (1–2 paras) — the actual BBC involvement: the kit, the workshop, the programme, the build. Specific and honest.
5. **The outcome** (1 para) — what actually happened. **Verified numbers only** (cross-checked against `Proof Bank`). No invented stats.
6. **A verified quote** — real, attributed, sourced (from `Blog Quotes & Testimonials` or a primary source). **Omit entirely if none can be verified** — never fabricate a quote.
7. **Why it matters** (1–2 sentences) — tie to the mission / funding loop without slogan-spam.
8. **Proof & links** — press coverage, partner pages, related stories.

## Voice & rules (non-negotiable)
- Person first · one story · numbers late · British English · warm, human, **no corporate jargon**.
- **Safeguarding:** never link a named/identifiable participant to a named prison; named Makers only with consent; never identify a minor.
- **Accuracy:** every name, number, date, partner and quote must trace to a primary source. The current articles are AI-generated and **assumed unreliable until verified**.
- **Strip-to-verified:** if a claim can't be verified, remove it. A shorter true story beats a longer embellished one.

## Verified-source priority (in order)
1. Vault `Business/Proof Bank.md` (numbers/facts), `Marketing/Blog Quotes & Testimonials.md` (quotes), `Marketing/Press and Media Log.md` (coverage), `Context/BBC Narrative.md` (canonical story).
2. Original press coverage on the web (partner/outlet sites — UCL, Design Museum, schools, named outlets).
3. Gmail threads with the partner/participant (original quotes, confirmations).
4. Topic-specific vault notes (grep the vault).

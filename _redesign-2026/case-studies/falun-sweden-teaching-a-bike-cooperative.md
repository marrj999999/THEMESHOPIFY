# Teaching a community bike cooperative in Falun, Sweden

A bike kitchen in the Swedish town of Falun cleared a workshop, set up our jigs, and spent the day learning to build bamboo bicycle frames from scratch.

## The context
Cykelköket Falun is a "bike kitchen" — a community bicycle cooperative where people fix, share and learn about bikes. It is exactly the kind of place our teaching is built for: there's no need for a formal institutional partnership, just a workshop space, willing hands and the right materials. In June 2018 we brought our portable teaching setup to Falun to show the cooperative how a bamboo frame comes together.

## What we did
We set up jigs on the cooperative's benches and worked through the frame-building process with the group — handling the bamboo, cutting and mitring tubes, and binding the joints. It is the same hands-on method we use everywhere from London to schools and prisons: no welding, no heavy machinery, just bamboo, a jig and good guidance. By the end, the group had the skills to keep building bamboo frames as part of what their cooperative offers.

## The outcome
A well-received community workshop and a genuine skills transfer — a Swedish bike cooperative left able to build with bamboo, adding to the worldwide network of people we've taught to make their own frames.

## In our own words
> "Great group and some real enthusiasm for bike building!"
> — James Marr, Bamboo Bicycle Club, June 2018

## Why it matters
Teaching abroad is how our method spreads beyond London. Each cooperative, school or community group we train becomes its own source of bamboo-building knowledge — part of how we've now reached makers across more than 36 countries.

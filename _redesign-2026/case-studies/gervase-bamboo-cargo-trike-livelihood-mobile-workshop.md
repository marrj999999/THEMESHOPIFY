# The bamboo e-cargo trike: when the bike can be the business

> Editorial note (not for publication): the original version of this post attributed this trike to a named customer, "Gervase", who supposedly designed and built it to carry a mobile chisel-sharpening workbench to craft fairs. That customer build could not be verified — and the photo used is in fact our own internal e-cargo R&D prototype ("LOAFLY"), the same image we use on several other pages. The verified-only version below tells the real, sourced story: our in-house e-cargo trike, and what a bamboo cargo bike can do for a small maker's livelihood. If we can confirm a genuine Gervase build with a real photo and quote, we can revive the customer framing.

## Context

A lot of small craft businesses quietly assume they need a van. Get to the fair, carry the workbench, the tools and the pop-up stand — and pay for the hire, the fuel and the low-emission-zone charge every time. We wanted to know whether a bamboo cargo bike could do that job instead.

## What we did

We designed and built a bamboo **e-cargo trike** in-house as an R&D project — a three-wheeled bamboo frame with a flat load platform, built from thick Guada bamboo so it carries weight without flexing. It started as a response to a neighbouring delivery business's needs, and grew into our reference design for what a bamboo cargo frame can do. The build was documented and shared, and the same platform has since fed into our university design-education work (UCL Masters projects and others).

## Verified outcome

- A working bamboo e-cargo trike, built and documented by Bamboo Bicycle Club.
- A reusable cargo-frame design that has gone on to support applied design-education projects with university students.
- A real-world demonstration that a bamboo frame can carry a serious load — a city's "van trips" (the school run, the last mile, the weekly shop) without the van.

## Verified quote

None available for a customer build. (Our published R&D framing: *"We embarked on a mission to design an e-cargo delivery bike from the ground up."*)

## Why it matters

For a small maker, the bike is not just transport — it is the shopfront. A hand-built bamboo cargo trike arriving at a market says something about craft and sustainability before a word is spoken. That is the genuine, demonstrable case for cargo bikes in small business, and it stands on its own without inventing the details.

# GCN builds and reviews a bamboo bike

The Global Cycling Network — one of the largest cycling channels in the world — came to
Bamboo Bicycle Club to build a bamboo frame from scratch and put it to the test.

Their presenters worked through the whole process: selecting the bamboo, setting up the
jig, and turning a bundle of canes into a complete, rideable frame. The finished gravel
build was set up for 650B × 47mm tyres and taken out across mixed terrain to see how it
holds up against the claims people make about bamboo as a frame material.

The takeaway was the one our builders have known for over a decade: a well-built bamboo
frame is a genuinely capable bike, with its own ride character — not a novelty.

Bamboo's appeal here comes down to comfort. As road.cc put it in their December 2020 build
feature, bamboo offers "excellent vibration dampening effects" alongside good stress
resistance and durability — which is exactly why it suits gravel, where the surface is
unpredictable and comfort over distance matters.

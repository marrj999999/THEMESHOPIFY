# UCL Master's students put bamboo through its paces

**Bamboo Bicycle Club set live engineering briefs for Master's students at University College London — testing what a bamboo frame can really carry, and what it can become.**

## The challenge

UCL's engineering Master's students needed real-world design briefs, and bamboo offered a properly awkward material to work with: non-standard, variable cross-sections, joined by hand rather than welded or moulded. That makes it a genuine engineering problem, not a craft exercise — exactly the kind of constraint that teaches young engineers to design around the material in front of them rather than the one in the textbook.

## What we did

We ran applied projects with UCL Master's students in Mechanical Engineering, giving each cohort a working brief and a frame to actually build and test. One project tested the structural properties of bamboo over six months and built a cargo bike rated to carry 150kg. Another produced a bamboo cargo bike designed to hold 100kg for agricultural use — load-carrying transport for low-resource settings, buildable with hand tools. A further year's brief took on an electric bamboo bike.

## The outcome

The students came away with hands-on experience of designing and load-testing a real frame in an unconventional material — and Bamboo Bicycle Club came away with structural insight that fed back into its own education and R&D work. The partnership is one strand of an education programme that now reaches schools and universities in the UK and abroad.

## In their words

> "Project with UCL, working with Master students on a 6-month project testing structural properties and building a cargo bike to hold 150kg. Our education programme has grown and we work with schools in the UK and abroad."
> — Bamboo Bicycle Club

## Why it matters

University partnerships like this one prove bamboo stands up as a serious engineering material — the same credibility that underpins Bamboo Bicycle Club's school programmes and its prison-rehabilitation and skills work.

# Designboom: build your own sustainable commute

On **26 January 2023**, design magazine **Designboom** featured the Bamboo Bicycle Club's
**Bamboo Gravel Bike Build Kit** — the kit that lets you build your own bicycle frame at home
from bamboo poles joined with recycled-aluminium lugs.

Designboom described how the kit lets riders "take control of their commute and become a maker
of their own vehicle", joining bamboo poles with recycled-aluminium lugs for a custom frame
with consistent performance — and noted that the pre-made connections "speed up the build
progress by 75%".

In Designboom's words:

> "Promoting local manufacturing, DIY, and independence, Bamboo Bicycle Club's Bamboo Gravel
> Bike Build Kit revolutionises our relationship with cycling."

> "By combining the lug with bamboo, it offers a natural vibration dampening and resolves the
> current bicycle market's lack of flexibility and high costs."

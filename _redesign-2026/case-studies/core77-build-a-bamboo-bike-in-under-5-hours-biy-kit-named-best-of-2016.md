# Featured in Core77: building the "Bike of the Future" at the London Design Museum

Industrial-design publication **Core77** covered Bamboo Bicycle Club's live build at the
**London Design Museum** in 2016 — a public demonstration of a bamboo bicycle frame joined
with **3D-printed lugs**, part of the museum's exhibition programme.

> Verified Core77 coverage: "Creating the 'Bike of the Future' in a Live 3D Printing and
> Bicycle Build at the London Design Museum" (Core77, 2016).

That build placed BBC's open-source, hand-built approach alongside some of the most talked-about
design objects of the year, and reflects what the workshops are really about: putting frame-building
tools in people's hands rather than selling a finished bike off a shelf.

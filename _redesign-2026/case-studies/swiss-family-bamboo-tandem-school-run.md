# A three-seat bamboo tandem, built at a kitchen table in Switzerland

**A home-build kit customer in Switzerland took our standard kit and turned it into something we'd never made before: a three-person bamboo tandem for the family school run.**

## The idea

Most people who order a home-build kit set out to make one bicycle for themselves. This builder had a different plan — a tandem long enough to carry the whole family, built at home, with no workshop visit and no prior framebuilding experience.

## What they built

Working from our home-build kit over several weeks, they adapted the standard design into a custom three-seat frame — each joint mitred, bonded and wrapped by hand. The finished tandem is wrapped in bright colour, one shade per section: red, orange, yellow, green and blue.

## How it's used

It now does the school run, carries the family's gear on holidays, and rolls through the Swiss countryside — a working family bicycle that started as a flat-pack of bamboo and a set of instructions.

## Why it matters

Switzerland is one of more than 36 countries where people have built a Bamboo Bicycle Club frame from the same kit and the same instructions as builders in London. A three-person tandem that no factory would tool up for is exactly the point: the method travels, and it bends to whatever the builder needs it to be.

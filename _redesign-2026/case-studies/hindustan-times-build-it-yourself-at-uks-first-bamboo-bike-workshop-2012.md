# The Guardian — our first national press

In November 2012, just two months after the Bamboo Bicycle Club launched, the Guardian's
Bike Blog ran a feature on our monthly workshops in Hackney Wick, east London. Written by
James Stewart, it was the first national press coverage we ever received.

Stewart spent a Saturday morning with builders mitring bamboo, setting tubes in jigs and
wrapping joints — and summed the day up in a line we still love:

> "Woodwork class was never like this."

At the time, a place on the course was £389 and included a computer-designed custom frame
and a weekend of building. The club was founded by James Marr and Ian McMillan, who had
spent years developing the bamboo frame-building process before opening it up to the public.

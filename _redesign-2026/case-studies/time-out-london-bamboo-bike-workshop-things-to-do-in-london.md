# Time Out London picked our bamboo bike workshop as a thing to do in London

Time Out London listed the Bamboo Bicycle Club workshop in its **Things to Do — Classes and Workshops** guide (dated 10 February 2016). Here is exactly what they wrote, in their words:

> "These two-day workshops teach participants how to make their very own bike frames using bamboo. The classes allow participants to be as creative as they wish, with help from expert frame builders who'll provide all the skills needed to create strong and sturdy structures to ride home on. Top up days are available if you want to add extra components and bamboo beer is supplied at the end of each day. Classes are for a maximum of four people and need to be booked in advance via the Bamboo Bicycle Club website."

That's the whole listing, and it still sums up the workshop nicely: a small group (maximum four people), two days at the bench with our frame builders, a frame you ride home on, and a bamboo beer to finish. The price at the time was £590.

# road.cc featured our Easy Build Kit

In its "Bike at bedtime" series, UK cycling publication **road.cc** featured the Bamboo Bicycle Club Easy Build Kit — the kit that cuts the time to build a bamboo road frame from around **70 hours** with our original kit down to roughly **4 hours**, a 75% reduction.

- **Outlet:** road.cc
- **Article:** "Bike at bedtime: Bamboo road bike made with the easy build home kit" by Anna Marie Hughes
- **Published:** 19 December 2020
- **Read the original:** https://road.cc/content/feature/bike-bedtime-bamboo-road-bike-easy-build-home-kit-279491

## What road.cc covered (verified)

- The **Easy Build Kit (£395)** uses pre-moulded carbon lugs, so you no longer have to build your own lug connections by hand — bringing the build down to about 4 hours.
- Our **original kit (£285)** remains the more hands-on, ~70-hour build.
- The carbon lugs weigh about 0.7kg and the bamboo around 1–1.5kg.
- The frame offers 32mm tyre clearance, mudguard and pannier mounts, internal cabling and disc-brake compatibility.
- road.cc noted bamboo's **"excellent vibration dampening effects"**, resistance to stress and durability.

Founded in London in 2012 by James Marr, Bamboo Bicycle Club helps people build their own sustainable bamboo bikes at home.

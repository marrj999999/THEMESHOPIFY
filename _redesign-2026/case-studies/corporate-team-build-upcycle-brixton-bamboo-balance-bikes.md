# A corporate team built balance bikes — then gave them to Brixton

A company came to Bamboo Bicycle Club for a team-building session and built bamboo balance bikes together. The finished bikes are being donated to **Upcycle**, a Brixton cycling charity, to help get more people riding.

## What we did
A corporate group spent the session building bamboo balance bikes by hand — measuring, cutting, sanding and binding the joints as a team. The point of a BBC corporate build is simple: people make something real together, and at the end there's a working bike to show for it.

## Where the bikes went
Rather than keep the bikes, the company chose to donate them to **Upcycle** (upCYCLE LDN), a Brixton-based charity that widens access to cycling — refurbishing and providing bikes, training and maintenance workshops for young people across south London. A corporate afternoon becomes a bike that keeps being ridden in the community.

## In the company's words
> "We just completed a team-building exercise with a company. The finished balance bikes will be donated to Upcycle, a charity in Brixton, to encourage cycling."

## Why it matters
A team build that ends in a donation gives a company two things at once: a shared, hands-on experience for the people who took part, and a real bike doing real good afterwards. No branded merchandise — just a working bamboo bike in someone else's hands.

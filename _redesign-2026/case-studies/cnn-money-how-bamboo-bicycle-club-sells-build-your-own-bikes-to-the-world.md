In October 2016, **CNN Money** featured Bamboo Bicycle Club in its Small Business section, under the headline *"Bamboo Bicycle Club sells kits that let you build your own bamboo bikes."*

CNN Money reported how the London-based club teaches people to build their own bespoke bamboo bike frames — first through in-person workshops, and later through home-build kits so anyone, anywhere, could build their own.

On bamboo as a frame material, CNN Money quoted co-founder James Marr directly:

> "The material provides natural suspension." — James Marr, engineer and co-founder, Bamboo Bicycle Club (CNN Money, 7 October 2016)

The article noted that the kits ranged from **$360 to $460**, in road, mountain bike and tandem designs, and that around **70% of kit sales went to customers outside the UK** — mostly in Europe, the United States and Australia.

Read the original article on CNN Money: [Bamboo Bicycle Club sells kits that let you build your own bamboo bikes](https://money.cnn.com/2016/10/07/smallbusiness/bamboo-bicycle-club/) (7 October 2016).

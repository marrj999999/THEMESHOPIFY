# The Guardian: "Hands-down one of the best things I have ever done"

In June 2015, Guardian writer **Stuart Heritage** spent a weekend at our Hackney Wick workshop building his own bamboo bicycle from scratch, and wrote it up for the paper's **"Homemade Life"** series (17 June 2015, photographs by Martin Godwin).

He arrived a sceptic and left with a frame he had cut, mitred and bound himself — and a verdict that has stuck with us ever since.

> "Going to the workshop was hands-down one of the best things I have ever done. Yes, at almost £500 it is a lot of money, but you are making a bespoke creation that is structurally sound and leaves a tiny carbon footprint."
>
> — Stuart Heritage, *The Guardian*, 17 June 2015

What we love most is what he wrote about the finished bike:

> "My bike is charmingly flawed... The bike is me. I do not think I could love it any more than I do."

That is the whole point of the Club: you do not buy a bamboo bike, you make one — a bespoke frame, designed to your measurements and built by your own hands over a weekend.

Read the full article on **[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2015/jun/17/homemade-life-bamboo-bicycle)**.

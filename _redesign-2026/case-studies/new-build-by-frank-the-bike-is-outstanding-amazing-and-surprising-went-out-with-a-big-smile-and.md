# Frank built his own bamboo bike — and rode off with a big smile

Frank built a bamboo bicycle from one of our home-build kits, and his verdict says everything we want a first ride to feel like.

## What Frank built
A flat-bar bamboo bike with golden bamboo tubes, black lugs and fork, and gum-wall tyres — finished off with the "Bamboo Bicycle Club" wordmark on the down tube. Like every kit build, the frame was made by hand from raw bamboo and natural-fibre joints.

## What we did
We don't sell finished bikes — we teach people to build them. Frank worked from a BBC kit and our tutorials to make the frame himself, then built it up into a complete, rideable bike.

## The outcome, in Frank's words
> "The bike is outstanding, amazing, and surprising. Went out with a big smile."

That feeling — the first ride on something you made yourself — is the whole point. We can supply the materials, teach the technique, and guide the process. The grin when you push off is yours.

## Why it matters
Since 2012, more than 4,000 people in over 36 countries have built their own bamboo bicycles with us. Frank's build is one of them: proof that a hand-built bamboo frame isn't a novelty — it's a bike people are genuinely proud to ride.

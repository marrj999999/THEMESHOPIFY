# We won Investec Beyond Business 2025

In September 2025, Bamboo Bicycle Club was named one of four winners of the Investec Beyond Business programme — Investec's social-enterprise accelerator, run in East London with the Bromley by Bow Centre.

## What the programme is

Beyond Business backs early-stage social enterprises in East London. This year it drew a record 148 applicants, narrowed to 11 finalists, who pitched their plans to a "Dragons' Den-style" panel on 25 September. From May to September, the cohort worked on their business plans alongside Investec experts and the team at the Bromley by Bow Centre.

## The result

Four winners were chosen — Bamboo Bicycle Club, Round Retail, Feeling Social and TrailFam — each receiving £24,000 in funding plus ongoing mentorship and business support from Investec colleagues.

We pitched Bamboo Bicycle Club as an organisation that reconnects excluded learners with education — delivering hands-on, accredited, industry-relevant programmes in sustainable design and mobility, opening pathways into further study, employment or enterprise.

## In their words

Ruth Leas, Chief Executive of Investec Bank, said: "I'm continually inspired by the creativity and determination shown by the social impact entrepreneurs who participate in the Investec Beyond Business programme."

Razia Nizamuddin, who leads the programme, added: "The entrepreneurial spirit of this year's winners is truly inspiring."

## Why it matters

The award is funding plus expertise. With it, we want to grow our community builds for schools, groups and rehabilitation settings, develop training pathways in sustainable making and bike mechanics, and keep pushing our circular, bio-based approach to building bicycles.

Thank you to Investec and the Bromley by Bow Centre.

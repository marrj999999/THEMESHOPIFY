# Bamboo Bicycle Club wins £24,000 in Investec's Beyond Business 2025

Bamboo Bicycle Club has been named one of four winners of **Investec's Beyond Business 2025** programme, earning a **£24,000 grant plus a year of business mentorship**. We were chosen from a record 148 applicants, with the winners decided by a Dragons' Den-style pitch.

## What the programme is

Beyond Business is run by Investec in partnership with the **Bromley by Bow Centre**, supporting early-stage social enterprises in East London. The 2025 cohort worked through the summer with Investec advisers on finance and marketing before pitching to the panel.

## The 2025 winners

Alongside Bamboo Bicycle Club, the cohort included **Round Retail, Feeling Social and TrailFam**. Across the four winners the programme awarded close to £100,000 in funding.

## Why we were chosen

In the words of the programme's own description of our work, Bamboo Bicycle Club:

> "reconnects excluded learners with education by delivering hands-on, industry-relevant, accredited programmes in sustainable design and mobility to open pathways into further study, employment or enterprise."

That spans our schools and education work, our corporate team-building, and our prison rehabilitation programme — all built around teaching people to design and build their own bamboo bicycle.

## What the funders said

Ruth Leas, Chief Executive of Investec Bank, said:

> "I'm continually inspired by the creativity and determination shown by the social impact entrepreneurs who participate in the Investec Beyond Business programme."

Razia Nizamuddin, the programme's leader, added that "the entrepreneurial spirit of this year's winners is truly inspiring."

## Why it matters to us

The £24,000 grant and the mentorship behind it help us put more makers through accredited, hands-on programmes — and move closer to a model that funds its social mission through earned income from workshops and kits, rather than grants alone.
